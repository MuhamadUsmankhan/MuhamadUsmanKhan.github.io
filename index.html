About Me

Turning CRM complexity into simple, automated experiences

Hi, I'm Muhammad Usman Khan, a Microsoft Certified (PL-600) Dynamics 365 CRM & Power Platform Consultant with 5+ years of experience and 20+ delivered projects across the USA, Europe, Australia and the Middle East.

I specialize in CRM customization, workflow automation and third-party integrations, working the full techno-functional spectrum from requirements and solution design down to code, extending the platform with C#/.NET plugins, custom APIs, JavaScript web resources and Azure Functions when low-code hits its ceiling.

Recently I architected a national-scale Dynamics 365/Dataverse investor portal with Power Pages self-service, server-side plugins and integration flows, and led pre-sales & solution design for government clients. I care about solutions that are scalable and maintainable, with proper ALM, clean solution layering, and integrations that hold up in production, not just demos.
